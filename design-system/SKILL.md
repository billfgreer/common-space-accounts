Key reminders for this brand:
- Link `styles.css` for tokens. Colors: black #000000, white #FFFFFF, cyan #00FCFA (signal), magenta #FF1DFF (alert), yellow #FFFF00 (UI highlight).
- Type: Archivo (heavy, uppercase display) + Space Mono (labels/data/captions).
- LIGHT BY DEFAULT: white ground almost always. Black is ink, not background — full-black surfaces are a rare, deliberate exception. For energy use a saturated yellow/cyan ground with black type.
- Text on cyan/magenta/yellow is ALWAYS black — those plates are too bright for white type.
- Square corners. Bordered flat cards (no shadows). ≈60 white / 25 black / 15 signal.
- Signature device: full-bleed CMY edge bars (cyan/magenta/yellow thirds) along one edge, in front of any halftone. Logo set large, top-left, over a black rule.
- Motifs: `///` eyebrows, `>>` forward chevrons, CMYK halftone bursts, signal dots. No emoji.
